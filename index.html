About

I'm a first year at Gies College of Business at the University of Illinois, and most of what I do sits somewhere between finance and code.

I co-founded MeridianAlgo, a nonprofit that builds open source finance tools, and I write a lot of the Python and JavaScript behind it. We also run financial literacy workshops for schools and youth groups, covering budgeting, investing, and staying out of debt.

On campus I cover commodity futures, options, and crypto for the Macro Investing Association and evaluate startups as an analyst with the Venture Capital Association. Before college I interned in finance at iTriangle Infotech in Bengaluru and taught kids to code at Code Ninjas.

I speak English and Kannada, and enough French to get by.
